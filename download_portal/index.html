⚡ How to Make CleanLink Appear First in Android's Share Sheet

Android uses machine learning heuristics and system ranking algorithms to order apps in the system share dialog. Follow these proven techniques to ensure CleanLink is always at your fingertips:

1. Pin CleanLink to the Front (Most Effective) 📌
Open any app (such as Chrome, YouTube, X/Twitter, or Instagram) > Tap Share > Locate CleanLink in the app carousel > Long-press the CleanLink icon > Tap Pin (📌).
Result: Android locks CleanLink permanently to the #1 first position in the share dialog across all apps on your device!
2. Built-In Direct Share Target Shortcut ⚡
CleanLink v1.8.0 implements Android's <share-target> specification via shortcuts.xml and publishes dynamic ShortcutInfoCompat targets. On Android 10, 11, 12, 13, and 14+, this qualifies CleanLink to appear in the prominent top-row Direct Share carousel.
3. Intent Filter Resolver Priority (android:priority=100) 🎯
CleanLink declares android:priority="100" for text/plain and text/* in its manifest, giving it higher resolver affinity on Samsung OneUI, Motorola, Xiaomi, and Pixel sharesheet implementations.
4. Usage Frequency & Recency Weighting 📈
Android's on-device UsageStatsManager tracks sharing frequency. Choosing CleanLink 2–3 times trains the on-device AI to rank CleanLink higher automatically whenever a link is shared.
