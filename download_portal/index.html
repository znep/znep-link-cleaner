⚡ How to Make ZnepLinkCleaner Appear First in Android's Share Sheet

Android uses machine learning heuristics and system ranking algorithms to order apps in the system share dialog. Follow these proven techniques to ensure ZnepLinkCleaner is always at your fingertips:

1. Pin ZnepLinkCleaner to the Front (Most Effective) 📌
Open any app (such as Chrome, YouTube, X/Twitter, or Instagram) > Tap Share > Locate ZnepLinkCleaner in the app carousel > Long-press the ZnepLinkCleaner icon > Tap Pin (📌).
Result: Android locks ZnepLinkCleaner permanently to the #1 first position in the share dialog across all apps on your device!
2. Built-In Direct Share Target Shortcut ⚡
ZnepLinkCleaner v2.0.0 implements Android's <share-target> specification via shortcuts.xml (`com.znep.linkcleaner.category.DIRECT_SHARE_TARGET`) and publishes dynamic ShortcutInfoCompat targets. On Android 10+, this qualifies ZnepLinkCleaner to appear in the prominent top-row Direct Share carousel.
3. Intent Filter Resolver Priority (android:priority=100) 🎯
ZnepLinkCleaner declares android:priority="100" for text/plain and text/* in its manifest, giving it higher resolver affinity on Samsung OneUI, Motorola, Xiaomi, and Pixel sharesheet implementations.
4. Usage Frequency & Recency Weighting 📈
Android's on-device UsageStatsManager tracks sharing frequency. Choosing ZnepLinkCleaner 2–3 times trains the on-device AI to rank ZnepLinkCleaner higher automatically whenever a link is shared.
