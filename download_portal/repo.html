  • Implemented UrlSecurityValidator and SafePublicDns in UrlExpander to block SSRF redirects against loopback, RFC1918 LAN, link-local, CGNAT, and cloud metadata IPs

  • Added anti-phishing & homograph detection that strips deceptive user:pass@host credentials and alerts on Punycode (xn--) lookalike domains, .apk/.exe downloads, raw IPs, and cleartext HTTP links
  • Added Offline-Only Mode toggle in Protection settings with on-demand shortlink expansion
